# Acceptable Use & Abuse Reports

## Acceptable Use Policy

Use of the eth.limo gateway is governed by our Acceptable Use Policy (AUP):

* [eth.limo Acceptable Use Policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15_AqiYzQngosXoJCvSOkFoiVYE6gduMP1JZ087JtgI0)

## Reporting Abuse

To report content or behavior that violates the AUP — phishing, malware, impersonation, or other abusive use of the gateway — use either channel:

* **Email**: [abuse@eth.limo](mailto:abuse@eth.limo)
* **Abuse intake form**: [https://forms.gle/n85iLgSuim9mmP6EA](https://forms.gle/n85iLgSuim9mmP6EA)

When reporting, please include the full URL(s) involved, a description of the issue, and any supporting evidence — this helps us triage and act quickly.

{% hint style="info" %}
For technical problems with your own dWebsite (resolution errors, configuration issues), use the [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) guide and the `#support-tickets` channel in Discord instead — the abuse channels are for AUP violations only.
{% endhint %}
