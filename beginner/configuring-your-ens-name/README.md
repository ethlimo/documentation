# Setting Up Your ENS Name/Domain

Once your content is published to decentralized storage, the final step is pointing your ENS name at it. This section covers:

* [Updating Your ENS Content Records](updating-your-ens-content-records.md) — the ENS Manager walkthrough for setting your content hash
* [Content Hash Overview](content-hash-overview/README.md) — what content hashes are and how IPFS, IPNS, and CIDs relate

After your content hash is set, your dWebsite is live at `https://yourname.eth.limo`.
